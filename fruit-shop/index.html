How it works

  1. Choose your fruit

    Pick single fruits or a weekly box. Prices are shown per pound or per item, so you always know what you pay.

  2. We pick and pack

    Orders are picked fresh the day before delivery and packed in recyclable paper and cardboard.

  3. It arrives next morning

    Delivered to your door before noon. Anything not perfect, we replace it or refund it.

Questions

When will my order arrive?

Orders placed before 6 pm arrive the next morning. We deliver Tuesday to Saturday.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is $4.99, and free when your order is $35 or more.

What if my fruit is not good?

Tell us within 24 hours and we will send a replacement or refund the item. You do not need to send anything back.

Can I skip or pause a weekly box?

Yes. You can skip a week, pause, or cancel at any time before the Sunday cutoff.

Is the fruit organic?

Some of it is, and it is marked on the product. We buy from growers we know and tell you where each fruit comes from.
